# Filesystem helpers

Use the target Neovim installation’s help for version-specific signatures and behavior (start with `:help vim.fs`). For other versions, consult the matching release’s documentation. The online [filesystem helpers documentation](https://neovim.io/doc/user/lua.html) tracks current Neovim and may differ from the target. Relevant help topics: vim.fs.
